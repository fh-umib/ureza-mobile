## 2. Project Idea

**Urëza turns a request from the Kosovo diaspora into a documented local visit, supported by GPS, live photos, voice evidence, and a clear report.**

The service will initially focus on property inspections and may also support consent-based, non-medical parent care visits.

## 3. The Problem

Diaspora members cannot always inspect their houses, apartments, or land in Kosovo. Many depend on verbal updates, relatives, or workers, making it difficult to discover damage quickly and verify completed work.

Our three interviews identified late information, incomplete paid services, inaccurate reports, costs, and remote coordination problems. See [BISEDAT.md](BISEDAT.md).

## 4. First Target User

**A Kosovo diaspora homeowner living abroad who needs a trusted person to inspect and report on a property in Kosovo.**

This is our first customer role. Managing businesses and multiple service providers is a possible later segment.

## 5. Proposed Solution

A client submits a visit request; **Miku**, a local visitor, accepts and performs it. Miku records GPS, live camera photos, voice notes, and timestamps. The client receives a structured report in their preferred language, then approves or disputes it.

AI report generation is planned to be simulated in the prototype. Approved reports are stored in **Oda**, the chronological visit history.

## 6. Initial MVP

The planned first version includes:

- Property inspections and optional non-medical parent visits (with consent)
- Basic Client and Miku roles, without real backend login
- Task creation, details, and status tracking
- Task acceptance by Miku
- GPS, live photos, voice recordings, and timestamps
- AI-generated reports in the client's language (**simulated in the prototype**)
- Client approval or dispute of reports
- Basic admin review of disputes
- Oda — chronological visit history

**Outside the initial MVP:** Company listings/offers, advanced business tools, online payments, push notifications, a public web app, real backend authentication, real AI integration, and medical advice.

**Current prototype:** Only the synthetic task list, local status changes, task details, and navigation are implemented. The rest is planned.
