## 1. The Five Pitch Elements

### Who

Kosovo diaspora members who own properties or have elderly parents in Kosovo but live abroad.

### Problem

They cannot regularly check their properties, verify completed work, or stay informed about important situations in Kosovo.

### Evidence

We interviewed three diaspora members, and all three described real problems:

- P1 spent three additional days cleaning land after a paid service left the work unfinished.
- P2 experienced inaccurate reports from workers managing his properties.
- P3 learned about a burglary at her house approximately one week after the incident.

These interviews demonstrate problems experienced by our participants, but broader market demand still requires validation.

### One Job

Urëza turns a request from the Kosovo diaspora into a documented local visit, supported by GPS, live photos, voice evidence, and a clear report.

### What We Need

Our initial pilot target is:

- 5 diaspora families with real property inspection needs.
- 3 trustworthy local visitors (Miku).
- Feedback from both sides to evaluate trust, usability, and service reliability.

The pilot is planned; participants have not yet been confirmed.

## 2. One-Minute Pitch — Albanian

### Flutura — Part 1

Përshëndetje! Ne jemi Flutura dhe Tringa, dhe projekti ynë quhet Urëza.

Urëza u shërben njerëzve të diasporës që kanë prona ose prindër në Kosovë, por jetojnë jashtë.

Intervistuam tre persona dhe të tre kishin probleme konkrete: njëri humbi tri ditë duke pastruar tokën pas një pune të papërfunduar, tjetri mori raporte të pasakta nga punëtorët, ndërsa një familje mësoi për vjedhjen e shtëpisë rreth një javë më vonë.

### Tringa — Part 2

Urëza bën një punë: e kthen një kërkesë nga larg në një vizitë të dokumentuar me dëshmi.

Klienti krijon kërkesën, Miku e pranon dhe regjistron GPS, foto live dhe zë.

Në versionin e planifikuar, klienti merr një raport në gjuhën e vet, me ndihmën e AI-së, dhe mund ta miratojë ose kundërshtojë.

Aktualisht kemi prototipin me listën dhe detajet e detyrave.

Për pilotin na duhen pesë familje nga diaspora dhe tre vizitorë lokalë të besueshëm.

## 3. Prototype Status

### Already Implemented

- Task list using `FlatList`.
- Reusable `TaskCard` (Karta e Detyrës) component.
- Local task status updates using `useState`.
- Dynamic task details with Expo Router.
- Navigation between the task list and details.
- Handling of invalid task IDs.

### Planned MVP

- Client request creation.
- Task acceptance by Miku.
- Property inspections and non-medical parent care visits.
- GPS, live camera photos, voice recordings, and timestamps.
- AI-generated reports in the client's language (simulated initially).
- Client approval or dispute of reports.
- Basic admin dispute review.
- Oda — chronological visit history.

The current prototype uses synthetic data. The remaining features are planned and are not yet functional.

**Pilot objective:**

**What we want to learn:**

1. Can clients create and understand an inspection request?
2. Can local visitors understand and complete assigned tasks?
3. Are GPS, live photographs, and voice evidence sufficient for clients to review the visit?
4. Can clients clearly approve or dispute a submitted report?
5. What should be improved before developing a real service?
